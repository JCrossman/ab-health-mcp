8. Data and Privacy

The desktop extension, download website, and separate remote server have different data flows. The experimental web-chat portal is disabled.

8a. Claude Desktop Extension (.mcpb path)

When you install the Extension via the downloadable .mcpb file and use it within Claude Desktop:

  • The Extension runs locally on your computer.
  • Your health data is fetched directly from Alberta's health portals to your machine. It does not pass through our servers.
  • Your health data is then sent to Anthropic's Claude AI (servers located in the United States) for the AI conversation. See Anthropic's privacy policy and their Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.
  • The MCP does not keep a health-record database. It stores encrypted sign-in cookies at ~/.mhr-records/session.enc and a dedicated Chrome profile at ~/.mhr-records/browser-profile. The profile can retain sign-in cookies and browser data. A session timeout is not a guarantee that those files have been erased.
  • Ordinary disconnect removes the saved MCP session, but the dedicated browser may remember Alberta sign-in. Local encryption does not protect against someone using the same operating-system account. Protect your computer and account.
  • Explicitly asking the extension to forget this device (forget_device: true) also removes its dedicated browser profile and saved sign-in files. The tool reports incomplete cleanup. This does not erase Claude history, normal browser data, or Alberta records, and is not a global Alberta sign-out.
  • You enter credentials on Alberta's own sign-in page. Do not enter passwords or health records into download links, support emails, or GitHub issues.
  • Claude Desktop and Anthropic manage your conversations, including health records returned to them. Retention and model-training rules depend on your account, settings, and their current policies. We do not promise a fixed retention period or manage, erase, or migrate Claude's history. Other MCP hosts have their own policies.

8b. Website and downloads

Downloads are public. You do not need to give us your name, email, or reason for downloading.

  • The access-request endpoint is retired. It no longer reads submitted details, sends mail, checks CAPTCHA, issues invitations, or writes access-request records. Old emailed download links redirect to the public installer without forwarding their token.
  • We removed browser analytics, CAPTCHA scripts, and custom download records containing IP addresses or browser details. The download API makes a read-only link that expires after 30 minutes.
  • Hosting, CDN, and storage services still receive IP addresses and request metadata, such as timestamps, browser headers, paths, and query strings. This can include tokens in old URLs or signed download URLs in platform logs. Removing application tracking does not remove those logs. Their retention depends on service settings and provider policies; we have not verified a single retention period for every copy.
  • The website loads Inter from Google Fonts. Links to Claude, GitHub, Alberta, and other sites take you to services with their own privacy rules. Our pages and download redirects use a no-referrer policy.
  • The extension's update check sends its installed version to our download API. It does not need health records, a name, or an email.

8c. Earlier access requests and emails

The previous download process collected names, email addresses, optional reasons, and download metadata in Azure Tables and notification emails. Reasons may have included health details. Copies may still exist in Tables, mailboxes, operational logs, and backups. Historical cleanup is pending separate approval, including any recordkeeping needs and email or backup administrator action. Stopping new collection does not erase those copies. We do not claim that historical data has been deleted.

8d. Remote server, disabled portal, and hosting regions

  • Public website: Azure Static Web Apps, Central US. A move to Canadian website hosting has not been completed.
  • Download storage and separate remote MCP server: Canada Central. Remote MCP requests, unlike desktop requests, pass health data through that server. Authentication state is encrypted in access tokens; this does not eliminate operational platform logs.
  • Web-chat portal: Disabled; web chat, sign-in, and beta invitations are unavailable. Earlier versions saved transcripts in browser storage; disabling the portal does not prove that earlier copies have been removed.
  • Dormant portal AI configuration: Azure AI Foundry Canada East. Any future activation requires a separate review of provider handling, abuse monitoring, retention, and authentication. See Microsoft's data privacy documentation.

A Canadian resource location is not a guarantee of all-Canadian processing or legal compliance. We do not claim HIA or POPA certification. Claude conversations, website delivery, and third-party services can involve processing outside Canada.
