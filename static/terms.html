8. Data and Privacy

Two delivery paths — different data flows

MyAI Health is offered through two distinct paths, each with a different data flow. Please read the section that applies to how you use the product.

8a. Claude Desktop Extension (.mcpb path)

When you install the Extension via the downloadable .mcpb file and use it within Claude Desktop:

  • The Extension runs locally on your computer.
  • Your health data is fetched directly from Alberta's health portals to your machine. It does not pass through our servers.
  • Your health data is then sent to Anthropic's Claude AI (servers located in the United States) for the AI conversation. See Anthropic's privacy policy and their Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.
  • Encrypted session cookies are stored locally on your machine only (AES-256-GCM). No health data is stored on your machine or our servers.
  • Your login credentials are entered on Alberta's own sign-in page and are never transmitted to or stored by us.

8b. Portal Beta (web chat path)

When you use the MyAI Health web portal (beta) at myaihealth.ca:

  • Your health data is fetched from Alberta's health portals via our server, which runs on Microsoft Azure Canada Central. Data passes through our infrastructure during the active session.
  • Your health data and conversation messages are sent to Azure OpenAI Service (Microsoft-hosted, Canada East region) for AI processing. All AI inference occurs within Canada.
  • Health data is held in memory only for the duration of your active session. It is not persisted to any database, log file, or long-term storage on our side.
  • Conversation transcripts are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). They are not stored on our servers. Clearing your browser data will remove them.
  • Encrypted session tokens (AES-256-GCM) are stored in your browser for the duration of the active session (~10 minutes before expiry). They contain authentication state only — no health data.
  • Your login credentials are entered on Alberta's own sign-in page and are never transmitted to or stored by us.
  • Azure OpenAI abuse monitoring: Microsoft Azure OpenAI may retain prompt and completion data for up to 30 days for abuse detection purposes, unless an opt-out has been approved. We do not use your data for AI model training. See Azure OpenAI data, privacy, and security.

8c. Canadian Data Residency (Portal Beta)

All infrastructure operated by MyAI Health for the Portal beta path is located in Canada:

  • MCP server / API: Microsoft Azure Canada Central
  • AI inference: Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service, Canada East
  • Website hosting: Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps (Canada Central)

This infrastructure is operated under contracts with Microsoft Azure in compliance with Alberta's Health Information Act (HIA) and Protection of Privacy Act (POPA).

Claude Desktop / .mcpb path exception: Conversations through Claude Desktop are processed by Anthropic on US-based servers. We do not control Anthropic's infrastructure or data handling. See Anthropic's policies for details.
