Your data stays yours

Built with privacy as a non-negotiable. No compromises.

No Data Storage

The extension never stores your health data. It's fetched from Alberta's portals and passed to Claude for your conversation, then discarded.

Encrypted Sessions

Session cookies are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored locally on your machine. No one else can access them.

Canadian Infrastructure

Our server infrastructure runs in Azure Canada Central. Your conversations are processed by Claude (Anthropic). No health data is stored by either system.

Credentials Never Stored

You sign in through Alberta's real portal in your browser. Your username and password are never seen or stored by the extension.

Read-Only Access

The extension can only read your records. It cannot modify, delete, or write anything to your health portals.

Add the free PubMed connector to Claude and search millions of medical research articles — guided by your own health records.

What is PubMed?

PubMed is a free database of over 36 million medical research articles. It's run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Doctors and researchers use it every day to find the latest studies and evidence. When you add it to Claude, you can search it too — just by asking.

Visit PubMed

Your health information stays private

When Claude searches PubMed, it only sends search words — like "high cholesterol treatment studies." Your name, health data, and personal details are never shared with PubMed. Claude uses your records to pick the right search words, but your private information never leaves your conversation.

Frequently asked questions

What is MyAI Health?

MyAI Health is a Claude Desktop extension that connects to Alberta's My Health Records portal and AHS MyChart (Connect Care). It lets you ask about your lab results, immunizations, medications, vitals, and appointments in plain language — no medical jargon required.

Is my health data stored or shared?

No. The extension never stores your health data. It fetches information from Alberta's official health portals for your conversation, then discards it. Only encrypted session cookies are stored locally on your machine. Your credentials are entered directly in Alberta's sign-in page and never touch the extension.

What do I need to use it?

You need the Claude Desktop app (macOS or Windows), a MyAlberta Digital ID with My Health Records access, and Google Chrome installed on your computer. No API keys, no terminal, no configuration files.

Does this provide medical advice?

No. MyAI Health is an information retrieval tool, not a medical device. It helps you access and understand your health records, but it does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or recommend treatments. Always consult your doctor or healthcare provider for medical decisions.

Which health portals are supported?

MyAI Health connects to two Alberta health portals: My Health Records (myhealthrecords.alberta.ca) for lab results, immunizations, medications, vitals, imaging, and procedures; and AHS MyChart / Connect Care (myahsconnect.albertahealthservices.ca) for appointments, messages, care team, allergies, clinical documents, and test results.

Is it free?

The MyAI Health extension is free. You do need a Claude Desktop subscription (from Anthropic) to use it. The extension itself is open source.
