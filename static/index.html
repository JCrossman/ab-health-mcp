Accessibility benefits

Health portals are complex — multiple logins, dense tables, medical jargon. Talking to AI takes away those problems and makes your health data easy to get to.

🎤

Voice interaction

Speak naturally to access your health data — no typing required. Claude Desktop supports voice input, helping people who have trouble moving, typing, or seeing.

📖

Plain language explanations

AI translates medical jargon into language you understand. Ask follow-up questions, learn about health problems, and go to your next visit ready with questions.

🧭

No complex navigation

One conversation replaces dozens of clicks across two separate portals. No menus, tabs, or page loads — just ask and get answers. Especially helpful for elderly users or anyone overwhelmed by complex interfaces.

🔊

Screen reader friendly

Claude's text-based responses work naturally with screen readers — unlike the busy portal pages full of charts and tables that screen readers have trouble with.

📋

Doctor visit preparation

Generate PDF summaries, organize health concerns, and prepare questions before appointments. Helpful for anyone who finds it hard to explain problems when rushed, feels nervous about health, or is dealing with a tricky health problem.

🌍

Multilingual support

Claude can explain your health data in languages other than English — important for Alberta's diverse communities where English may not be a first language.

👨‍👩‍👧

Help for caregivers

Family members managing health for elderly parents or dependents can quickly pull a full picture across all their records — labs, medicines, appointments, referrals — without having to learn two different websites.

Your data stays yours

We built this to keep your data safe. No exceptions.

No Data Storage

The extension never stores your health data. It's fetched from Alberta's portals and passed to Claude for your conversation, then discarded.

Encrypted Sessions

Your login session is locked with strong encryption (AES-256-GCM) and saved only on your computer. No one else can access them.

Canadian Infrastructure

Our servers run in Azure Canada Central. Your conversations are processed by Claude (Anthropic). No health data is stored by either system.

Login Details Never Stored

You sign in through Alberta's real portal in your browser. Your username and password are never seen or stored by the extension.

Read-Only Access

The extension can only read your records. It cannot modify, delete, or write anything to your health portals.

Research what your results mean

Add the free PubMed connector to Claude and search millions of medical research articles — guided by your own health records.

What is PubMed?

PubMed is a free database of over 36 million medical research articles. It's run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Doctors and researchers use it every day to find the latest studies and evidence. When you add it to Claude, you can search it too — just by asking.

Visit PubMed

Your health information stays private

When Claude searches PubMed, it only sends search words — like "high cholesterol treatment studies." Your name, health data, and personal details are never shared with PubMed. Claude uses your records to pick the right search words, but your private information never leaves your conversation.

2

Frequently asked questions

What is MyAI Health?

MyAI Health is a Claude Desktop extension that connects to Alberta's My Health Records portal and AHS MyChart (Connect Care). It lets you ask about your lab results, vaccines, medicines, vitals, and appointments in plain language — no medical jargon required.

Is my health data stored or shared?

No. The extension never stores your health data. It gets your info from Alberta's health websites for your chat, then throws it away. Only locked login files are saved on your computer. Your username and password are entered directly in Alberta's sign-in page and never touch the extension.

What do I need to use it?

You need the Claude Desktop app (macOS or Windows), a MyAlberta Digital ID with My Health Records access, and Google Chrome installed on your computer. No special codes, no command line, no setup files.

Does this provide medical advice?

No. MyAI Health is a tool that finds your health info. It is not a medical device. It helps you access and understand your health records, but it does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or recommend treatments. Always consult your doctor or healthcare provider for medical decisions.

Which health portals are supported?

MyAI Health connects to two Alberta health portals: My Health Records (myhealthrecords.alberta.ca) for lab results, vaccines, medicines, vitals, imaging, and procedures; and AHS MyChart / Connect Care (myahsconnect.albertahealthservices.ca) for appointments, messages, care team, allergies, doctor's notes, and test results.

Is it free?

The MyAI Health extension is free. You do need a Claude Desktop subscription (from Anthropic) to use it. The extension itself is open source.
